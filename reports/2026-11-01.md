## Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL)

- **Report period:** Q3 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026), reported October 28, 2026
- **Revenue:** $127.6B, up 25% YoY — the company's 13th straight quarter of double-digit growth. Google Services revenue $96.3B (+11% YoY); Google Cloud revenue $30.7B, up 103% YoY (more than doubling), with TPU systems sales cited as a meaningful contributor.
- **Margins:** Consolidated operating margin 31.8% per the press release (operating income up 30% YoY). CNBC reports operating income of $40.58B, missing a $43.19B FactSet forecast. Google Cloud segment operating income was approximately $11.1B, a ~36.2% segment margin, per one SEC-filing analysis (unconfirmed against a second source).
- **Net income/EPS:** GAAP net income available to common stockholders $26.2B; GAAP diluted EPS $2.12 (down from $2.87 a year earlier). The decline reflects an approximately $8.7B loss on equity securities this quarter (primarily the company's SpaceX investment stake), versus a large gain in the year-ago quarter. CNBC separately cites an "adjusted" EPS of $2.67 against a $3.02 estimate — this adjusted figure is not fully reconciled with the GAAP figure across sources reviewed.
- **Free cash flow:** Negative $9.065B for the quarter (operating cash flow $42.29B; capex $51.36B, below the ~$55.09B analysts expected). Trailing-twelve-month free cash flow was $19.747B. Quarterly FCF trend per one analysis: +$24.6B (Q4 2025) → +$10.1B (Q1 2026) → -$5.9B (Q2 2026) → -$9.1B (Q3 2026).
- **Balance sheet:** One secondary (unverified) source puts cash and marketable securities at $265.1B and long-term debt at $125.1B as of September 30, 2026; not corroborated against the primary SEC filing from sources reviewed this run.
- **Guidance/commentary:** Full-year 2026 capex guidance held unchanged at $195B–$205B; management said 2027 capital spending will rise significantly further, without giving a specific figure.
- **Stock performance:** Shares fell more than 10% on the earnings reaction (October 28, 2026) as the market weighed held-steady 2026 capex against a flagged, larger 2027 increase. No reliable November 2026 closing price or forward P/E was found from sources reviewed.
- **Sentiment:** Currently seeing heightened retail investor and social-media discussion, tied to the Cloud growth beat, the equity-securities-driven EPS swing, and the sharp post-earnings stock drop.
- **Sources:** SEC 8-K (sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001652044/000165204426000080/googexhibit991q32026.htm), CNBC (10/28/2026 earnings report), Alphabet Investor Relations Q3 2026 earnings call page, FourWeekMBA, vestedfinance.com, a Substack cash-flow analysis (captainharris.substack.com), AltIndex Reddit-mention and WSB trackers.

- **Revenue:** Net sales $201.9B, up 12% YoY (13% ex-currency), roughly in line with the ~$202B consensus. AWS revenue approximately $46.0B (one source: $45.98B), up 39% YoY — its fastest growth in nearly five years, slightly ahead of the ~38% analysts expected.
- **Margins:** Operating income rose 61% YoY to a reported company record operating margin (exact consolidated margin percentage not confirmed from sources reviewed). AWS operating income $17.8B, a margin of approximately 38.7%.

- **Free cash flow:** Trailing-twelve-month free cash flow was an outflow of $42.4B, a 386% YoY deterioration, driven by a roughly $94.8B YoY increase in property-and-equipment purchases tied to AI infrastructure. Q3 capex cited as $77.3B (CNBC) or $75.9B (alternate analysis) — not reconciled.
- **Balance sheet:** Sources conflict: one analysis reports cash and marketable securities of $114.2B at September 30, 2026; another reports cash and cash equivalents alone fell from $86.8B (year-end 2025) to $55.8B — likely a marketable-securities classification difference, not reconciled from sources reviewed. Long-term debt rose to $173.1B from $65.6B at year-end 2025, after raising $115.1B in long-term borrowing over the first nine months of 2026, including $48.1B in Q3 alone.
- **Guidance/commentary:** Q4 2026 net sales guided to $240B–$247B, with operating income guided to $28.5B–$33.5B. Management (per one transcript) projected AWS growth exceeding 40% in both 2027 and 2028. Full-year 2026 capex outlook held at approximately $220B.

## Intel Corporation (INTC)

- **Report period:** Q3 2026 (quarter ended September 26, 2026), reported October 29, 2026
- **Revenue:** $16.5B, up 21% YoY. Sources disagree on whether this beat expectations — one cites a beat against a $16.44B estimate, another says it fell just short of a $16.54B estimate.
- **Margins:** Non-GAAP gross margin 44.1%, roughly 200 basis points above both Q2 2026 and the company's own guidance. Data center and AI revenue was $7.3B, up 17% sequentially and nearly 80% YoY; Client Computing revenue was $8.1B, down 9% sequentially and below internal expectations.
- **Net income/EPS:** GAAP diluted EPS attributable to Intel $0.60 (versus $0.90 a year earlier). Non-GAAP EPS $0.52, beating the company's own guidance of $0.38 and a consensus estimate of roughly $0.4059.
- **Free cash flow:** Adjusted free cash flow $1.639B, up from $896M a year earlier. GAAP operating cash flow $5.082B; gross capex for the quarter $4.219B. Year-to-date: operating cash flow $13.2B, capex $10.2B.
- **Balance sheet:** Cash and cash equivalents $21.846B (versus $14.265B a year earlier); cash plus short-term investments $54.0B at quarter-end, substantially boosted by a $23B public equity offering completed during the period. Total debt not confirmed from sources reviewed.
- **Guidance/commentary:** Q4 2026 revenue guided to $15.0B–$16.0B, with GAAP EPS guidance of $0.10 and non-GAAP EPS guidance of $0.25 — guidance came in below analyst expectations and weighed on the stock. 2026 gross capex remains on track at approximately $20B; management expects 2027 capital spending to rise further with negative free cash flow. CEO Lip-Bu Tan projected substantial multi-year growth in the server CPU market; CFO David Zinsner flagged a declining PC market and continued weak cash flow. Bank of America and Roth Capital both downgraded the stock to Neutral around the same period.
- **Stock performance:** Shares were reported under pressure following the softer-than-expected Q4 guidance. No reliable November 2026 closing price, market cap, or forward P/E was confirmed from sources reviewed this run (conflicting undated figures for forward P/E ranged roughly 70x–137x across secondary sites).
- **Sentiment:** Currently seeing heightened retail investor and social-media discussion, tied to the Q3 beat, the disappointing Q4 outlook, and ongoing debate over Intel's AI/data-center positioning following its large equity raise.
