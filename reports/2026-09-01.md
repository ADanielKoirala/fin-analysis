- **Stock performance:** ~$220.78 as of September 1, 2026 — roughly flat (~+0.4%) over the trailing month per one source. Forward P/E estimates vary meaningfully by source/date: figures found ranged from ~18x to ~25.6x.
- **Sentiment:** Elevated retail/social discussion, consistent with its status as the most closely watched AI-infrastructure stock.
