## Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL)

- **Report period:** Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026), reported in the week of July 22–23, 2026 (sources differ by a day on the exact date).
- **Revenue:** $119.8B, up 24.2% YoY, beating consensus estimates of roughly $113.6B–$116.5B. Google Cloud revenue was $24.8B, up 81.8% YoY and about 10% above consensus.
- **Margins:** Consolidated operating margin approximately 34%, short of a pre-earnings consensus estimate near 35.3% as AI infrastructure spending accelerated. Google Cloud segment operating margin rose to 35.6% from 20.7% a year earlier, with segment operating income roughly tripling from $2.8B to $8.8B.
- **Net income/EPS:** GAAP net income available to common stockholders of approximately $112.1B; GAAP diluted EPS $9.11, far above the ~$2.87–$2.89 consensus. The beat was driven almost entirely by a roughly $99.0B non-cash gain on equity securities (an investment stake), not core operating results — not comparable to consensus on a like-for-like basis.
- **Free cash flow:** Approximately negative $5.9B for the quarter — described by multiple sources as Alphabet's first negative quarterly free cash flow since its 2004 IPO (one source instead frames it as the first in at least a decade). Capex was a record $44.9B, exceeding the $39.1B generated from operations.
- **Balance sheet:** Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled approximately $242.5B as of June 30, 2026. Total debt cited in the range of roughly $112.9B–$120.8B across sources, not fully reconciled; one analysis notes Alphabet still held an estimated $121.7B in net cash.
- **Guidance/commentary:** Full-year 2026 capex guidance raised to $195B–$205B, up from a prior $180B–$190B range. Management said 2027 capital spending would rise significantly further without giving a specific figure; outside analyst estimates for 2027 capex ranged from roughly $265B to $350B. The CFO said free cash flow would remain under pressure from continued infrastructure investment. One report cited $85B raised through combined debt and equity issuance in June, earmarked for AI infrastructure through 2027.
- **Stock performance:** Approximately $351.40 as of September 21, 2026, with market capitalization cited near $4.25T. No reliable October 2026 price or forward P/E was found from sources reviewed.
- **Sentiment:** Elevated retail/social discussion, tied to the large headline EPS beat (driven by a one-time gain), strong Cloud growth, and debate over the scale of AI capital spending.
- **Sources:** SEC 8-K (sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001652044/000165204426000066/googexhibit991q22026.htm), beancount.io Alphabet Q2 2026 earnings analysis, S&P Global Market Intelligence, tikr.com, dev.to (Gentic News), fierce-network.com, aiweekly.co, pluang.com.

No reliable October 2026 closing price or forward P/E was found from sources reviewed.

## Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN)

- **Report period:** Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026), reported July 30, 2026.
- **Revenue:** Net sales $200.6B, up 20% YoY from $167.7B — the first time Amazon's quarterly revenue crossed $200B. AWS revenue was $42.2B, up 37% YoY (its fastest growth in 18 quarters, versus roughly 31% expected). Advertising revenue rose 26% to $19.8B.
- **Margins:** Consolidated operating margin 13.7%, up from 11.4% YoY, on operating income of $27.5B (+43% YoY). By segment: North America operating margin ~7.9% ($9.1B operating income on $116.2B sales, +16% YoY); International operating margin ~4.1% ($1.7B operating income on $42.2B sales); AWS operating margin 39.4% (up from 32.9%), with AWS operating income of $16.6B (+64% YoY), aided by an estimated 520-basis-point one-time benefit from energy-derivative accounting. Gross margin is not an Amazon-reported figure; one third-party estimate puts it near 52% ($104.8B of estimated gross profit).
- **Net income/EPS:** Net income $62.6B, GAAP diluted EPS $5.75 (versus $1.68 a year earlier) — not comparable to the ~$1.82 consensus because the result included a $53.4B non-operating pre-tax gain, primarily from Amazon's investment in Anthropic.
- **Free cash flow:** Trailing-twelve-month free cash flow was an outflow of $7.6B, driven by AI-related capital spending.
- **Balance sheet:** Cash and equivalents $78.2B as of June 30, 2026 (down from $86.8B at year-end 2025), plus $44.8B in separate marketable securities. Total long-term debt (face value) was approximately $133.0B, up sharply from $68.8B at year-end 2025, following debt issuance in multiple currencies in March 2026; carrying value was approximately $128.9B.
- **Guidance/commentary:** Q3 2026 net sales guided to $197B–$202B, with operating income guided to $22.5B–$26.5B. The 2026 capital expenditure outlook was raised to approximately $220B.
- **Stock performance:** Approximately $255.49 as of September 21, 2026 (up 0.70% that day), with market capitalization cited near $2.74T. No reliable October 2026 closing price or forward P/E was found from sources reviewed.
- **Sentiment:** Elevated retail/social discussion, tied to AWS's reaccelerating growth, the headline net-income beat (aided by the Anthropic gain), and continued AI capex scrutiny.
- **Sources:** SEC 8-K/10-Q (sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001018724/000101872426000024 and .../000101872426000026), Quartr Q2 2026 earnings summary, pulse2.com, qz.com, beancount.io Amazon FY2026 Q2 earnings analysis, Nasdaq press release, rivkin.com.au, stockanalysis.com, pluang.com.
