## GameStop Corp. (GME)

- **Report period:** Q2 2026 (13 weeks ended August 1, 2026), reported September 8, 2026
- **Revenue:** $790.2M net sales, down 18.7% YoY, but above the ~$756.8M analyst consensus. Decline attributed to a prior-year Nintendo Switch 2 launch comp, planned store closures, and the divestiture of France operations.

Operating income $160.2M, a record for the quarter, implying an operating margin of roughly 20.3%. SG&A fell to $187.1M from $218.8M YoY. Margin expansion driven by a mix shift toward higher-margin Collectibles (57% YoY growth in that segment, now 45.1% of net sales).

Adjusted (non-GAAP) EPS $0.27, above the ~$0.19 consensus. Adjusted net income $161.1M (up from $138.3M YoY); Adjusted EBITDA $174.0M vs. $75.7M a year ago.
- **Free cash flow:** Not fully confirmed from sources reviewed — one secondary (non-filing) source cited Q2 operating cash flow of $62.4M and capex of $1.7M, implying FCF of roughly $60.7M, but this could not be verified against the primary SEC filing in this run.
- **Balance sheet:** Company-stated cash, cash equivalents, marketable securities, and digital assets/related receivables totaling $5.4B, plus a separate $4.9B investment in eBay Inc. common stock, as of August 1, 2026. A conflicting secondary-source cash figure (~$8.69B) appeared inconsistent/likely erroneous and was disregarded. Total debt not confirmed from sources reviewed.
- **Guidance/commentary:** Full-year fiscal 2026 Adjusted EBITDA outlook raised — cited as "in excess of $650M" by one source and "~$600M" by another (possibly reflecting sequential guidance raises between quarters rather than a contradiction; exact current figure not fully confirmed). Management commentary continues to emphasize the pending/proposed eBay-related deal; CEO Ryan Cohen reportedly gave up a performance-based pay package to focus on it.
- **Stock performance:** Traded roughly $18.50–$19.89 in early-to-mid September 2026; 52-week range approximately $18.55–$28.03 (as of early August 2026). Exact early-October closing price not confirmed. Forward P/E estimates diverged widely across sources (11.95, 16.32, 28.25) and were not reconciled.
- **Sentiment:** Elevated retail/social discussion, amplified by the earnings beat, the eBay equity stake, and ongoing acquisition speculation.
