Currently seeing heightened retail investor and social-media discussion, tied to continued outsized AI/data-center demand and anticipation ahead of the upcoming Q3 report.

~$92.3B, up 19% YoY, above the ~$90.63B consensus. Azure revenue ~$33.2B, up 48% YoY, beating both the company's 44–45% growth guidance and external estimates.

Free cash flow
$18.6B, down 27% YoY, as capex growth outpaced a 29% rise in operating cash flow (to $58.0B). Management expects to stay free-cash-flow positive in fiscal 2027.
Cash & debt
Cash and short-term investments fell $12.3B over the quarter, partly from a $4.9B debt repayment. Total cash and total debt balances not confirmed from sources reviewed.
Stock performance
Rose roughly 4% on the earnings reaction (Oct. 28, 2026). Most recent dated pre-earnings snapshot (Sept. 4, 2026): ~$499.70, market cap ~$3.71T, trailing P/E 28.4. No reliable November 2026 closing price was found.

Guidance & commentary

Azure revenue growth for fiscal Q2 2027 guided to 49–50%. Quarterly capital expenditures were approximately $45.2B.

Currently seeing heightened retail investor and social-media discussion, tied to the Azure growth beat and continued focus on AI-driven capital spending.

Guidance & commentary

Currently seeing heightened retail investor and social-media discussion, tied to the Cloud growth beat, the equity-securities-driven EPS swing, and the sharp post-earnings stock drop.

Amazon.com, Inc.

AMZN

Q3 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026) — reported October 29, 2026

Revenue & YoY growth
Net sales $201.9B, up 12% YoY (13% ex-currency), roughly in line with the ~$202B consensus. AWS revenue ~$46.0B, up 39% YoY — its fastest growth in nearly five years.
Gross / operating margin
Operating income rose 61% YoY to a reported company-record operating margin (exact consolidated percentage not confirmed). AWS operating income $17.8B, margin ~38.7%.
Net income & EPS
Net income $20.7B, down from $21.2B a year ago (prior-year quarter included large one-time gains). GAAP diluted EPS $1.89, missing a consensus cited as $1.96–$1.97.
Free cash flow
Trailing-twelve-month free cash flow was an outflow of $42.4B (down 386% YoY), driven by a ~$94.8B YoY rise in AI-related capex. Q3 capex cited as $75.9B–$77.3B across sources.
Cash & debt
Sources conflict on cash: $114.2B in cash and marketable securities per one analysis, versus cash and equivalents alone falling to $55.8B per another. Long-term debt rose to $173.1B from $65.6B at year-end 2025, after $115.1B in new borrowing over nine months (including $48.1B in Q3).
Stock performance
Fell after hours following the report, with cited moves ranging roughly 6%–11% depending on the source. No reliable November 2026 closing price or forward P/E was found.

Guidance & commentary

Q4 2026 net sales guided to $240B–$247B, operating income guided to $28.5B–$33.5B. Full-year 2026 capex outlook held at approximately $220B; management projected AWS growth exceeding 40% in both 2027 and 2028.

Currently seeing heightened retail investor and social-media discussion, tied to AWS's accelerating growth, the EPS miss, and scrutiny of rising debt-funded AI capital spending.

Intel Corporation

INTC

Q3 2026 (quarter ended September 26, 2026) — reported October 29, 2026

Revenue & YoY growth
$16.5B, up 21% YoY. Sources disagree on whether this beat or slightly missed consensus (estimates cited $16.44B–$16.54B).
Gross / operating margin
Non-GAAP gross margin 44.1%, ~200bps above Q2 2026 and above the company's own guidance. Data center & AI revenue $7.3B (+17% sequentially, ~+80% YoY); Client Computing $8.1B (-9% sequentially).
Net income & EPS
GAAP diluted EPS $0.60 (vs. $0.90 a year ago). Non-GAAP EPS $0.52, beating the company's own $0.38 guidance and a ~$0.4059 consensus.
Free cash flow
Adjusted free cash flow $1.639B, up from $896M a year earlier. Operating cash flow $5.082B; gross capex for the quarter $4.219B.
Cash & debt
Cash and equivalents $21.846B (vs. $14.265B a year ago); cash plus short-term investments $54.0B, substantially boosted by a $23B equity offering completed in the quarter. Total debt not confirmed from sources reviewed.
Stock performance
Shares were reported under pressure after the softer Q4 guidance. No reliable November 2026 closing price, market cap, or forward P/E was confirmed (undated secondary estimates for forward P/E ranged widely, roughly 70x–137x).

Guidance & commentary

Q4 2026 revenue guided to $15.0B–$16.0B, with GAAP EPS guidance of $0.10 and non-GAAP EPS guidance of $0.25 — below analyst expectations. 2026 gross capex remains on track at ~$20B; management expects 2027 capex to rise further with negative free cash flow. The CEO projected substantial multi-year growth in the server CPU market; two analyst firms downgraded the stock to Neutral around the same period.

Currently seeing heightened retail investor and social-media discussion, tied to the Q3 beat, the disappointing Q4 outlook, and debate over Intel's AI/data-center positioning following its large equity raise.
