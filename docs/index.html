Alphabet Inc.

GOOGL

Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026) — reported late July 2026

Revenue & YoY growth
$119.8B, up 24.2% YoY, beating consensus estimates of roughly $113.6B–$116.5B. Google Cloud revenue $24.8B, up 81.8% YoY.
Gross / operating margin
Consolidated operating margin ~34%, short of a pre-earnings estimate near 35.3% amid rising AI infrastructure spending. Google Cloud segment operating margin rose to 35.6% from 20.7% a year ago.
Net income & EPS
GAAP net income ~$112.1B; GAAP diluted EPS $9.11, far above the ~$2.87–$2.89 consensus — driven almost entirely by a ~$99.0B non-cash equity-securities gain, not core operating results.
Free cash flow
Approximately $(5.9)B for the quarter — described by multiple sources as Alphabet's first negative quarterly free cash flow since its 2004 IPO. Capex was a record $44.9B, exceeding the $39.1B generated from operations.
Cash & debt
Cash, equivalents and marketable securities ~$242.5B as of June 30, 2026. Total debt cited in the range of roughly $112.9B–$120.8B across sources, not fully reconciled.
Stock performance
Approximately $351.40 as of September 21, 2026, with market cap cited near $4.25T. No reliable October 2026 price or forward P/E was found from sources reviewed.

Guidance & commentary

Full-year 2026 capex guidance raised to $195B–$205B, from a prior $180B–$190B. Management said 2027 spending would rise significantly further without giving a figure; outside analyst estimates for 2027 capex ranged roughly $265B–$350B. The CFO said free cash flow would remain under pressure from continued infrastructure investment.

Currently seeing heightened retail investor and social-media discussion, tied to the large headline EPS beat (driven by a one-time gain), strong Cloud growth, and debate over AI capital spending.

Cash and equivalents $78.2B as of June 30, 2026 (down from $86.8B at year-end 2025), plus $44.8B in marketable securities. Total long-term debt (face value) ~$133.0B, up sharply from $68.8B at year-end 2025.
Stock performance
Approximately $255.49 as of September 21, 2026, with market cap cited near $2.74T. No reliable October 2026 closing price or forward P/E was found from sources reviewed.

Guidance & commentary

Q3 2026 net sales guided to $197B–$202B, with operating income guided to $22.5B–$26.5B. The 2026 capital expenditure outlook was raised to approximately $220B.

Currently seeing heightened retail investor and social-media discussion, tied to AWS's reaccelerating growth, the headline net-income beat, and continued AI capex scrutiny.
